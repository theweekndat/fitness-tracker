## Corrections to the brief found so far

1. **Cache API does nothing on `*.workers.dev`.** As I understand Cloudflare's docs, `caches.default` has no
   effect on workers.dev subdomains, and a custom domain is out of scope. So Open Food Facts responses are
   cached in **KV** instead: 30 days for hits, 1 day for not-found, never for 5xx/429. KV's free tier allows
   1,000 writes a day, which is far above what one person scanning needs. Confirm after deploy:
   the second lookup of a barcode should return `x-hub-cache: hit`.
2. **Rounding in the worked example.** Fat 25% × 2,408 kcal = 66.9 g; carbs come out at 298.25 g if fat is
   rounded to 67 g first, and 298.5 g otherwise. The golden vector C298 only holds with "round fat, then solve
   carbs". Proposed rule for Phase 1: round P and F to whole grams, then compute C from the rounded values.
3. **The "within 1 kcal" property test** cannot hold for whole grams in general (4 and 9 kcal steps).
   Proposed: exact match before rounding, ±5 kcal after rounding.
4. **Open Food Facts rate limit.** The brief says 15 product reads per minute; my recollection is 100 per minute for
   product reads and 10 per minute for search. Not verified (docs unreachable from the build environment).
   It barely matters with the KV cache.
5. **iOS web push** for Home-Screen web apps has been supported since iOS 16.4, so it should work; the device
   test confirms it.
6. **Fewer manual secrets:** the Worker generates its VAPID key pair and stores it in KV, so there is no
   VAPID secret to create.
